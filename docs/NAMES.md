# Name Decision

Selected working name: `Thoughtforge`

Status:

- approved for planning and implementation
- keep as a working brand until formal trademark, domain, and package-name clearance is complete

## Alternatives Considered

These names aim for "second brain", memory, intent, and agent collaboration without sounding like a clone of Obsidian.

## 1. Mneme

Why it works:

- strong memory association
- short and distinctive
- fits a second-brain product

Risk:

- requires explanation for some users

## 2. Thoughtforge

Why it works:

- feels active and agentic
- implies shaping ideas into outcomes

Risk:

- slightly more builder-oriented than calm knowledge-oriented

## 3. Intent Garden

Why it works:

- emphasizes growing ideas into plans
- human and agent collaboration vibe

Risk:

- softer tone than a power-tool brand

## 4. Cortex Dock

Why it works:

- implies a place where cognition connects to tools and systems

Risk:

- more technical, less poetic

## 5. Memory Mesh

Why it works:

- good for graph-based knowledge and agent retrieval

Risk:

- may sound more like infrastructure than an app

## 6. Loombook

Why it works:

- evokes weaving thoughts into a structured book of knowledge
- memorable and brandable

Risk:

- less explicit agentic feel

## 7. Threadvault

Why it works:

- combines durable storage with connected thinking
- good fit for notes plus agent conversations plus intent threads

Risk:

- slightly utilitarian

## 8. Palimpsest

Why it works:

- beautiful concept for layered memory and rewritten thought
- premium feel

Risk:

- hard to spell for many users

## 9. Synarchive

Why it works:

- mixes synthesis and archive
- fits a second-brain plus retrieval product

Risk:

- can sound archival rather than alive

## 10. Wayfinder

Why it works:

- implies orientation, decision-making, and guided action
- friendly for a broader audience

Risk:

- broader and less note-specific, so trademark screening matters

## Shortlist

Selected:

1. Thoughtforge

Strong runners-up:

1. Mneme
2. Threadvault

## Naming Notes

- Avoid names that are visually or phonetically too close to "Obsidian".
- Prefer a name that can support both knowledge management and agent execution.
- Run trademark, domain, and package-name checks before locking anything.
